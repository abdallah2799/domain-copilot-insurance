# Standard Exclusions Addendum — PAP-EXCL-2025

**Applies to:** Policy Form PAP-2025-STD
**Effective:** June 1, 2025

This addendum sets out exclusions applicable to all coverage parts of Policy Form PAP-2025-STD, incorporated by reference into the Policy Wording under Part F. It supersedes PAP-EXCL-2024 for policies written on the newer form.

## 1. Racing or Speed Contests

We do not provide coverage for loss occurring while the covered auto is used in an organized racing, speed, or timed driving event, or while practicing for such an event, whether or not on a closed course.

## 2. Intentional Loss

We do not provide coverage for loss intentionally caused by or at the direction of an insured, including damage caused with the intent to defraud.

## 3. Wear, Tear, and Mechanical Breakdown

We do not provide coverage for loss due to normal wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or road damage to tires, unless the damage results from a covered theft or a covered Collision or Comprehensive loss otherwise described in Part D.

## 4. Undisclosed Business Use

We do not provide coverage for loss arising out of the use of the covered auto to carry persons or property for a fee, including but not limited to delivery or courier services, unless a business-use endorsement specific to that use is attached to the policy and listed on the Declarations page. This exclusion does not apply to Ride-share/TNC use, which is addressed separately in Exclusion 9 below.

## 5. Driving Under the Influence

We do not provide coverage for loss occurring while the operator of the covered auto is under the influence of alcohol or a controlled substance in violation of the law of the state in which the accident occurred.

## 6. Unlisted Excluded Driver

We do not provide coverage for loss while the covered auto is operated by a person specifically excluded from coverage under this policy by a named-driver exclusion endorsement.

## 7. War and Nuclear Hazard

We do not provide coverage for loss due to war (declared or undeclared), insurrection, rebellion, or nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive contamination, however caused.

## 8. Racing, Rental, or Livery Fleets

We do not provide coverage while the covered auto is used as part of a rental fleet, livery fleet, or is available for hire to the general public as part of a fleet of three or more vehicles under common ownership or management.

## 9. Ride-share/TNC Use Without Endorsement

We do not provide coverage for bodily injury or property damage, and Part D does not apply to loss to the covered auto, arising out of Ride-share/TNC use — operating the covered auto while logged into a transportation network company application in driver mode, from the moment the app is set to available through the end of a trip — unless the **Ride-share/TNC Endorsement (Form END-RS-01)** is attached to the policy and listed on the Declarations page. This exclusion applies regardless of whether a passenger is present in the vehicle at the time of loss. See Policy Wording PAP-2025-STD, Section 7.6, for the corresponding coverage-part language.

---

*This exclusion is new as of Policy Form PAP-2025-STD. Claims under Policy Form PAP-2024-STD involving ride-share use are evaluated under Exclusion 4 (Undisclosed Business Use), not under a named ride-share exclusion — the underlying coverage outcome may differ depending on which form version governs the policy period in effect at the time of loss.*
