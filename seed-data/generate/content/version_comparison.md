# Policy Form Version Comparison — PAP-2024-STD vs. PAP-2025-STD

**Document ID:** MMIC-VERSION-COMPARE-2025
**Purpose:** Side-by-side reference for the four substantive differences between the two Policy Form editions in this corpus. This document does not itself grant or deny coverage — the governing Policy Wording and the Declarations page in force on the date of loss always control; see Adjudication Guidelines Step 1.

## 1. Comprehensive Glass Deductible

| | PAP-2024-STD | PAP-2025-STD |
|---|---|---|
| Rule | Standard Comprehensive deductible applies to all Comprehensive losses, including glass. | $0 deductible waiver for glass-only Comprehensive claims under $1,500 (Section 5.4). |
| Citation | Policy Wording Section 5.2 | Policy Wording Section 5.4 |
| Worked example | See Appendix, PAP-2024-STD, Example 2 | See Appendix, PAP-2025-STD, Example 2 |

## 2. Transportation Expense (Rental Reimbursement) Limit

| | PAP-2024-STD | PAP-2025-STD |
|---|---|---|
| Daily limit | $30/day | $45/day |
| Maximum days | 30 days (unchanged) | 30 days (unchanged) |
| Citation | Policy Wording Section 5.3 | Policy Wording Section 5.3 |
| Note | The Rental Reimbursement Increase Endorsement (END-RR-01) can further increase this to $60/day regardless of which form version applies. | |

## 3. Ride-share/TNC Use

| | PAP-2024-STD | PAP-2025-STD |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment | No dedicated provision; ride-share use is addressed under the general Undisclosed Business Use exclusion (PAP-EXCL-2024, Exclusion 4) unless separately endorsed. | Explicit Ride-share/TNC exclusion (PAP-EXCL-2025, Exclusion 9; Policy Wording Section 7.6), removable only by the Ride-share/TNC Endorsement (END-RS-01). |
| Practical effect | Coverage outcome for an undisclosed ride-share loss may differ between the two forms even with identical facts, since the exclusions are worded differently and reference different endorsement mechanics. | |

## 4. Claim Acknowledgment Timeline

| | PAP-2024-STD | PAP-2025-STD |
|---|---|---|
| Timeline | 15 business days | 10 business days |
| Citation | Policy Wording Section 8.1 | Policy Wording Section 8.1 |
| Regulatory alignment | Predates the reference timelines in the Ohio Regulatory Reference. | Matches the Ohio Regulatory Reference, Section 2. |

## 5. Provisions Unchanged Between Versions

The following are identical in substance between the two form versions and are not affected by which one governs a claim: Definitions (Section 1, except the added Ride-share/TNC Use definition in PAP-2025-STD), Liability Coverage (Part A), Medical Payments Coverage (Part B), Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist Coverage (Part C), Duties After an Accident or Loss (Part E, except the added ride-share disclosure duty in PAP-2025-STD Section 6.8), Appraisal (Section 8.2), Other Insurance (Section 8.3), Subrogation (Section 8.4), Loss Payee provisions (Section 8.5), Cancellation and Non-Renewal (Section 9), and Arbitration (Section 10).
