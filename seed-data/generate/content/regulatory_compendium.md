# Ohio Auto Insurance Regulatory Compendium

**Document ID:** MMIC-REG-COMPENDIUM-2025
**Purpose:** A consolidated, expanded regulatory reference for Meridian Mutual's Personal Auto line, drawing together and extending the topic-specific regulatory references elsewhere in this corpus.
**Effective:** January 1, 2025

*This document is a synthetic reference prepared for a software assessment corpus. It is presented for realism and internal consistency with this corpus only and should not be relied on as current legal advice.*

## 1. Financial Responsibility

Ohio requires liability insurance no less than $25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident bodily injury and $25,000 property damage, as summarized in the Ohio Regulatory Reference. All policyholders in this corpus carry limits at or above this minimum; see each policyholder's Declarations page.

## 2. Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Coverage

Ohio insurers are not required to include UM/UIM coverage automatically but must offer it in writing; a policyholder may reject it in writing. Every policyholder in this corpus has elected UM/UIM coverage. The Uninsured Motorist Property Damage Endorsement (END-UMPD-01) is a separate, optional extension beyond the bodily-injury-focused UM/UIM coverage in Policy Wording Part C.

## 3. Claims-Handling Timelines

### 3.1 Acknowledgment

10 business days under PAP-2025-STD, 15 under PAP-2024-STD, per Policy Wording Section 8.1 and the Ohio Regulatory Reference — extendable by up to 5 additional business days during a declared catastrophe event per the Catastrophe Claims Surge Protocol.

### 3.2 Investigation and Decision

An insurer should begin investigation within 15 business days of acknowledgment and communicate a coverage decision within 30 calendar days of completing investigation, per the Ohio Regulatory Reference, Section 2.

### 3.3 Payment After a Decision

An undisputed claim is paid within 10 business days of the coverage decision.

## 4. Unfair Claims Settlement Practices

Ohio prohibits a defined set of unfair claims settlement practices, detailed in the Ohio Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Reference, including misrepresenting policy provisions, failing to acknowledge claims promptly, denying a claim without reasonable investigation, and failing to explain a denial in writing with the specific policy provision relied upon.

## 5. Total Loss and Salvage Title

A vehicle for which a total loss claim is paid must be branded with a salvage title unless repaired to a roadworthy standard before resale, per the Ohio Total Loss and Salvage Title Requirements. The insurer must notify the state title office within 5 business days of a total loss settlement.

## 6. Data Privacy

While not exclusively an insurance-specific requirement, an insurer handling policyholder and claimant PII is expected to apply minimum-necessary access principles and document what data leaves its infrastructure to any third party, including a hosted AI service — see the Data Privacy and PII Handling Policy.

## 7. Producer (Agent) Conduct

A producer's binding authority, and the underwriting referral triggers that limit it, are documented in the Producer Manual Excerpt. Ohio requires a licensed producer for any binding transaction; this compendium does not restate licensing requirements themselves, which are administered separately from claims and coverage.
