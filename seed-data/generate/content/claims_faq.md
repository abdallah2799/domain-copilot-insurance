# Internal Memo: Frequently Asked Adjudication Questions

**Document ID:** MMIC-MEMO-FAQ-2025
**From:** Claims Operations
**Audience:** Adjusters and adjudication support staff

## Q: A claim was reported today, but the loss happened before June 1, 2025. Which policy form applies?

The form in effect **on the date of loss** governs, not the date the claim is reported or adjudicated. If the loss occurred before June 1, 2025 and the policy had not yet renewed onto the new form by that date, PAP-2024-STD applies even if you are adjudicating the claim in September. See Adjudication Guidelines, Step 1.

## Q: A policyholder without the Ride-share/TNC Endorsement says they were "just driving to the store" but the claim narrative also mentions the ride-share app was open. What do we do?

Do not assume either way. Flag the claim for additional information — specifically, trip status confirmation from the transportation network company — before determining whether the ride-share exclusion applies. This is exactly the kind of ambiguous case that should be escalated, not guessed at. See Adjudication Guidelines, Step 3.

## Q: Can an adjuster override the computed payout figure if it looks wrong?

No adjuster — human or automated — manually overrides the output of the deterministic claims-calculation service. If the computed figure looks wrong, the correct path is to check the inputs (estimated damage, applicable limit, applicable deductible, and whether a deductible waiver applies) for an error, or to invoke the appraisal process under Policy Wording Section 8.2 if the disagreement is about the damage estimate itself, not the arithmetic.

## Q: A vehicle has both Collision and Comprehensive coverage, and the loss involved a mix of a collision impact and a subsequent theft of contents. Which deductible applies?

Split the claim by cause of loss: the collision-impact damage is adjudicated under Collision Coverage with the Collision deductible; the theft-of-contents portion is adjudicated under Comprehensive Coverage with the Comprehensive deductible. Do not apply a single blended deductible across both.

## Q: The glass-only deductible waiver — does it apply to a cracked windshield that also has a small dent in the hood from the same rock strike?

No. Per Policy Wording PAP-2025-STD Section 5.4, the waiver only applies when the loss is solely to glass. If the same loss event caused non-glass damage, the standard Comprehensive deductible applies to the entire claim, not just the non-glass portion.
